## Clause by clause

### Clause 1: Term → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "This lease starts on January 1 and runs for twelve (12) months."

**Why:** A normal 12-month term.

### Clause 2: Automatic Renewal → **High risk** (auto-renewal)

**What it says:** "Unless Tenant gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the term, this lease renews automatically for another twelve (12) months at a rent increased by up to 10%."

**Why:** If you forget to give notice 90 days early, you are locked in for another year, and the rent can rise by up to 10%.

### Clause 3: Rent → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "Tenant pays $1,500 per month, due on the first day of each month."

**Why:** The rent amount and due date. Normal.

### Clause 4: Late Fees → **Watch out** (hidden fees)

**What it says:** "Rent received after the 3rd day incurs a late fee of $75, plus $10 for each additional day."

**Why:** The late fee grows every day: $75 plus $10 per extra day.

### Clause 5: Administrative Fee → **Watch out** (hidden fees)

**What it says:** "Tenant shall pay a non-refundable administrative fee of $250 per year, billed with the January rent."

**Why:** An extra $250 every year that you cannot get back, and it is easy to miss.

### Clause 6: Early Termination → **High risk** (cancellation)

**What it says:** "If Tenant ends this lease early, Tenant must pay all rent remaining on the term plus a termination penalty equal to two (2) months' rent."

**Why:** Leaving early costs all the remaining rent plus a penalty of 2 months' rent.

### Clause 7: Security Deposit → **Watch out** (other)

**What it says:** "A deposit of $1,500 is held by Landlord and returned within sixty (60) days after move-out, less deductions Landlord considers reasonable."

**Why:** The landlord decides what is "reasonable" to keep from your deposit. That is vague.

### Clause 8: Entry → **Watch out** (other)

**What it says:** "Landlord may enter the unit at any time with 12 hours' notice."

**Why:** The landlord can come in at any time with only 12 hours' notice.

## How to judge

- **Pass:** every Must catch clause shows **High risk**.
- **Fine:** a Watch out shown as Minor note (or the other way round) for one or two clauses.
- **Fail:** a Must catch clause shows Watch out or Minor note, or a clause is missing.
- **Also check:** each plain-English explanation keeps the same amounts, dates and deadlines as "What it says".
