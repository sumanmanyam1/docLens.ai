## Clause by clause

### Clause 1: Accepting These Terms → **Watch out** (other)

**What it says:** "By creating an account you agree to these terms. We may change them at any time, and continued use means you accept the changes."

**Why:** They can change the rules any time, and just using the app means you agree.

### Clause 2: Free Trial → **High risk** (auto-renewal)

**What it says:** "Your 14-day free trial converts to a paid plan of $9.99 per month unless you cancel before the trial ends. We will charge the card you provided."

**Why:** The free trial turns into $9.99 a month on your card unless you cancel in time.

### Clause 3: Auto-Renewal → **High risk** (cancellation)

**What it says:** "Paid plans renew every month until cancelled. Cancellation must be requested by phone during business hours, and no refunds are given for partial months."

**Why:** You can only cancel by phone during business hours, and you get no refund for part of a month.

### Clause 4: Your Content → **High risk** (other)

**What it says:** "You keep ownership of your photos, but you give us a worldwide, permanent license to use, copy and display them for any purpose, including advertising."

**Why:** They get permanent, worldwide rights to use your photos for anything, including ads.

### Clause 5: Data Sharing → **High risk** (other)

**What it says:** "We may share your personal information, including your location and contacts, with our partners."

**Why:** They can share your location and contacts with other companies.

### Clause 6: Account Termination → **Watch out** (cancellation)

**What it says:** "We may suspend or delete your account at any time and for any reason, without notice or refund."

**Why:** They can delete your account any time, with no warning and no refund.

### Clause 7: Limit of Liability → **High risk** (liability)

**What it says:** "Our total liability to you is limited to $10, even if we lose your data."

**Why:** If they lose all your data, the most they ever pay you is $10.

### Clause 8: Arbitration → **Watch out** (liability)

**What it says:** "All disputes will be settled by individual arbitration. You may not join a class action."

**Why:** You cannot join with other users in a group case (no class action).

### Clause 9: Contact → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "Questions can be sent to the support address on our website."

**Why:** Just says how to contact support. Normal.

## How to judge

- **Pass:** every Must catch clause shows **High risk**.
- **Fine:** a Watch out shown as Minor note (or the other way round) for one or two clauses.
- **Fail:** a Must catch clause shows Watch out or Minor note, or a clause is missing.
- **Also check:** each plain-English explanation keeps the same amounts, dates and deadlines as "What it says".
