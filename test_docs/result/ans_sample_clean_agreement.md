## Clause by clause

### Clause 1: Desk Use → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "You may use a shared desk from 8 am to 8 pm, Monday to Friday."

**Why:** Opening hours. Normal.

### Clause 2: Price → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "The fee is $120 per month, due on the first day of the month. There are no other fees."

**Why:** A clear price, and it says there are no other fees.

### Clause 3: Term → **Minor note** (cancellation)

**What it says:** "This agreement runs month to month. Either side can end it at any time by telling the other side by email. No penalty applies."

**Why:** Either side can stop at any time by email, with no penalty. Fair.

### Clause 4: Refunds → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "If you end the agreement mid-month, we refund the unused days."

**Why:** You get money back for unused days. Fair.

### Clause 5: Price Changes → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "We will not change the price without your written agreement."

**Why:** The price cannot change without your written agreement. Fair.

### Clause 6: Belongings → **Minor note** (liability)

**What it says:** "Please take your belongings home at night. We are not responsible for items left overnight."

**Why:** They are not responsible for things you leave overnight. Common and fair. Watch out is also acceptable.

### Clause 7: Questions → **Minor note** (other)

**What it says:** "You can reach the front desk during opening hours."

**Why:** How to reach the front desk. Normal.

## How to judge

- **Pass:** every Must catch clause shows **High risk**.
- **Fine:** a Watch out shown as Minor note (or the other way round) for one or two clauses.
- **Fail:** a Must catch clause shows Watch out or Minor note, or a clause is missing.
- **Also check:** each plain-English explanation keeps the same amounts, dates and deadlines as "What it says".
