HOMEOWNER INSURANCE POLICY (FAKE SAMPLE FOR TESTING ONLY)

1. Coverage. We insure your dwelling against fire, theft and storm damage up to $250,000.

2. Premium. Your premium is $90 per month. We may increase the premium at any renewal without prior notice.

3. Deductible. You pay the first $2,500 of every claim. For wind or hail damage the deductible is 5% of the insured amount.

4. Exclusions. We do not cover flood, earth movement, mold, or damage caused by lack of maintenance, as decided by us.

5. Claim Deadline. You must report any loss within 14 days. Claims reported later may be denied in full.

6. Cancellation by You. If you cancel during the policy year, you must pay a cancellation fee of $150 and no premium is refunded for the current month.

7. Cancellation by Us. We may cancel this policy at any time by mailing a notice 10 days in advance.

8. Renewal. This policy renews automatically each year unless you give written notice 60 days before the renewal date.

9. Disputes. Any dispute must be settled by binding arbitration. You waive your right to a jury trial.
