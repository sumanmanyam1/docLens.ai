RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (FAKE SAMPLE FOR TESTING ONLY)

1. Term. This lease starts on January 1 and runs for twelve (12) months.

2. Automatic Renewal. Unless Tenant gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before the end of the term, this lease renews automatically for another twelve (12) months at a rent increased by up to 10%.

3. Rent. Tenant pays $1,500 per month, due on the first day of each month.

4. Late Fees. Rent received after the 3rd day incurs a late fee of $75, plus $10 for each additional day.

5. Administrative Fee. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable administrative fee of $250 per year, billed with the January rent.

6. Early Termination. If Tenant ends this lease early, Tenant must pay all rent remaining on the term plus a termination penalty equal to two (2) months' rent.

7. Security Deposit. A deposit of $1,500 is held by Landlord and returned within sixty (60) days after move-out, less deductions Landlord considers reasonable.

8. Entry. Landlord may enter the unit at any time with 12 hours' notice.
