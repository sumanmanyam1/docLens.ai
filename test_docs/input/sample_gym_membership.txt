FITZONE GYM MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT (FAKE SAMPLE FOR TESTING ONLY)

1. Membership. You get access to the gym during opening hours for 12 months at $35 per month.

2. Joining Fee. A one-time joining fee of $99 is charged on signup and is not refundable.

3. Annual Fee. An annual maintenance fee of $59 is billed in month 6 of every year.

4. Automatic Renewal. After 12 months the membership continues month to month. Cancelling requires a signed letter sent by registered mail 30 days before the next billing date.

5. Early Cancellation. If you cancel before the 12 months end, you owe 50% of the remaining monthly fees.

6. Price Changes. We may raise the monthly fee with 7 days' notice posted on our notice board.

7. Freezing. You may freeze your membership for up to 3 months for a $20 monthly freeze fee.

8. Injuries. You use the gym at your own risk. The gym is not responsible for any injury, even if caused by faulty equipment.

9. Guests. Each member may bring one guest per month at no charge.
